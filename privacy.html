TokDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

TokDesk is a dashboard that lets creators view their own TikTok profile information, follower and like counts, and public video list.

Information we access

With your permission through TikTok Login Kit, TokDesk reads your display name, avatar, profile details, account statistics and public videos. Access is limited to the scopes you approve.

How we use it

The data is used only to show you your own account information inside TokDesk. We do not sell, share or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to provide the service. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact the site owner through the GitHub repository for this site.
